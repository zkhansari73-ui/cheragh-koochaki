Leo's Super Fuel Adventure
A fun story about a playful boy who discovered the secret power of food!

Five-year-old Leo was faster than a race car. "Vroom! Zoom!" he shouted, zooming his red sneakers across the living room rug.

"Breakfast time, Leo!" Mom called with warm oatmeal and berries. Leo giggled, "No way, Mom! Space rockets don't need breakfast; they run on pure speed!"

At lunchtime, Mom served crunchy carrots and toast. Leo ducked behind the couch. "I'm full already! My belly is stuffed with happy giggles!"

That afternoon, Leo ran to the sunny park. His best pals, Mia and Toby, were already warming up for their big game: Dinosaur Tag!

"You're it, Leo!" yelled Toby, sprinting across the grass. Leo laughed and revved up his sneakers. "I am Turbo T-Rex! You can't outrun me!"

Suddenly, Leo's turbo legs felt as heavy as stone. His zoom turned into a slow turtle walk. He could barely lift his favorite sneakers!

Leo plopped down on the grass. His friends zoomed far ahead. Right then, his tummy let out a loud, grumpy complaint: Grumble-squeak-rumble!

Mom came over with a gentle smile and knelt beside him. "What happened, Captain Turbo?" Leo frowned. "Mom, my rocket motor stopped working."

"Your motor isn't broken, Leo," Mom chuckled softly. "Even the fastest race car needs a pit stop. Food is your body's super fuel!"

"Cheese and eggs build strong jumping muscles. Crunchy fruits build your health shield, and bread gives your legs running power!"

Leo looked at a shiny red apple slice. He took one brave little bite. CRUNCH! Sweet juice splashed on his tongue. Next, he tried a savory cube of cheese.

A few minutes later, warm energy sparkled in Leo's knees. He bounced high on his toes and cheered, "My fuel tank is refilled! Turbo Rex is back!"

At dinner, Leo didn't eat everything. But he proudly ate three little broccoli trees and two bites of chicken. "Every rocket needs its super fuel!" he smiled. The End.
